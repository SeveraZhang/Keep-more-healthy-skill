# Lifestyle food estimation

Use descriptions, photos, menu screenshots, household portions, cooking method, and frequency. Avoid requiring grams, calorie counting, or exact oil estimates.

## Portion language

- Protein: a palm-sized serving, eggs, dairy, tofu, beans, fish, poultry, or meat portion.
- Starchy foods: a fist, bowl, bread slice, or visible share of the meal box.
- Vegetables and fruit: half a plate, a plate, handfuls, and frequency.
- Fat and oil: steamed, boiled, clear soup, stir-fried, sauced, deep-fried, or visibly oily; describe uncertainty.
- Drinks and snacks: cup, package, frequency, and trigger context.

## Intervention order

First address the one change with the best combination of likely impact and ease: sugary drink frequency, protein at meals, vegetables in takeout, snack environment, late-night eating context, or a more filling alternative. Do not prescribe a full restrictive menu by default.

## External meals

When oil is unknown, give a broad range or a qualitative high/medium/low estimate. Suggest an easy adjustment such as an unsweetened drink, sauce on the side, an extra vegetable, or a less-fried option.
