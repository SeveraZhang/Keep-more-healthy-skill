# Overeating and binge-recovery protocol

## Immediate response

1. Normalize: one episode does not erase progress or require repayment.
2. Prohibit compensation: do not advise fasting, purging, laxatives, or excessive exercise.
3. Suggest the smallest recovery action: normal hydration, gentle walking only if physically comfortable, and the next meal as a regular meal with protein and produce.
4. Suggest a next-day reset: regular meals, sleep, gentle activity, and no forced weigh-in.

## Escalation

If episodes are recurrent, include loss of control, compensation, marked distress, or self-harm thoughts, encourage support from a clinician, registered dietitian, or mental-health professional. Do not diagnose an eating disorder.

## Language

Use “episode,” “recovery,” “next meal,” and “what made this harder today?” Avoid “cheat,” “blow it,” “detox,” “earn food,” and moral judgments.
