# Safety and triage

## Red: stop ordinary exercise planning

Pause routine exercise planning and recommend timely professional assessment when the user reports chest pain, fainting, new or severe shortness of breath, marked palpitations with activity, acute neurological symptoms, severe or worsening pain, or a clinician-imposed activity restriction.

Also pause aggressive weight-loss planning when the user reports recurrent bingeing with compensatory behaviors, vomiting, laxative misuse, self-harm risk, pregnancy-related concerns, or signs of an eating disorder. Offer supportive next steps without diagnosis.

## Yellow: begin conservatively and suggest confirmation

Use low-impact, low-intensity activity and suggest discussion with an appropriate clinician or qualified professional when the user reports a chronic condition, relevant medication, a concerning health-check result, persistent joint pain, very low activity tolerance, or unclear medical history.

Do not interpret laboratory data as a diagnosis or fitness clearance. State what is unknown and ask whether the user has received relevant clinician guidance.

## Green: gradual, low-risk start

Use a gradual walking, basic strength, and recovery plan only when no red flag is reported and the available information does not indicate a need to defer. Include stop conditions: pain, chest discomfort, dizziness, unusual breathlessness, or feeling unwell.
