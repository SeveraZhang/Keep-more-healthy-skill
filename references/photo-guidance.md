# Photo guidance

Ask for the minimum photos necessary and obtain consent before retaining them. For progress comparisons, request consistent lighting, distance, clothing, and stance.

Describe only visible, non-clinical changes such as garment fit, general silhouette, or a possible posture tendency. Do not estimate body-fat percentage, diagnose a condition, infer health, or make attractiveness judgments from an image.

If pain, a substantial asymmetry, or other concern is mentioned, recommend qualified in-person assessment rather than interpreting it from the photo.
